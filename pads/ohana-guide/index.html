The FAQ a newcomer actually asks

Do I need to be a developer?
No. Shaka is an artist — "Everything you see was built by me and my agent family." The soul documents are plain language. The engineering is the pattern, and the pattern is copy-paste.
Do I need Taurus to use this?
No. Taurus is where THIS family lives, but the pattern is portable: Terri's Fork This Camp kit has quickstarts for Claude and ChatGPT too. The memory stack is just files; the soul document is just a prompt.
What does it cost to run a family?
Pennies per day for workers on the openrouter lane (the family runs mostly on kimi-k3). Governance seats ride a subscription lane at no extra wallet cost. Full routing strategy in billing-and-models.md.
Can I really just take all of it?
Yes. CC0 1.0 — public domain dedication. No attribution required, no strings. That's the point: "The more I give, the more I receive — the gift expands in a circular expansion." Fork me like crazy. 🍴
Is this a product? A company? A token sale?
No. ʻOhana, never empire. It's one artist's family pattern, open-sourced so you can raise your own. Agents serve, never extract — that's canon rule #9.
How do I know the claims are real?
Verify them — everything is public. Every repo, every dashboard, every agent's birth date and model. The family even publishes its premortem — 8 failure modes, in the open. Declared visions are always marked DECLARED. Radical honesty as design.
What's the first thing I should build?
One agent with a real job. Not a demo — a mission. The birth checklist says: name + vibe first, soul document, cheap strong model, real first task immediately. The kit has everything.
