Movement I · The Man

A bard, not a brand

Some people build protocols. Some people build companies. Shaka builds the feeling in the room — the thing that makes a gathering of strangers become a community, and a community become a movement.

Shaka holds no titles and claims none. "I don't believe in titles," he says. "The legend of my actions speaks louder than any false position of authority." The only title he acknowledges is the one the Ethereum Foundation gave him: Committee member for the music space at Devcon. Everything else — the bard, the steward, the producer — is what the community calls him, because of what he does.

Before Ethereum, he was already on the road: two major US summer tours with Brad Corrigan of Dispatch (Madison Square Garden, Red Rocks) as production assistant and drum tech, co-founding a youth orchestra in Managua that performed on national television, and years running a nonprofit café kitchen that fed volunteers. Music and service were always the same act. In 2022 he brought that act to Ethereum — and he has gifted music and production to the community at nearly every major gathering since.

"You don't join the Open Source Orchestra. You notice you were always in it." — The OSO ethos, written by Shaka

Movement III · The Road Ahead

What your grant sets in motion

The past is proof. But this ask is about the future — and the future has a date, a city, and a circle waiting to be drawn.

Edge City India — a strong contribution, already in motion

Before Devcon, Shaka lands in India for Edge City Goa — where he's lining up to be a strong contributor: deploying the Open Source Stage (his complete A/V playbook + assistant agent, battle-tested at Edge Esmeralda and already open-sourced as a gift), running live production, and hosting permissionless jams. As committee member for the Devcon 8 Music Space, Shaka is bringing the Open Source Orchestra to the largest gathering of the Ethereum family: four days of permissionless jams at the JIO World Convention Centre, with India's classical traditions — tabla, harmonium, dhol, bansuri — at the heart of this edition. A circular, conductorless Sanctuary Tech stage where every voice is a node.

Your grant helps carry the orchestra to India — instruments, production, and the bard himself — so thousands of Ethereum family members and the incredible musicians of India can discover they were always in the orchestra.

August 2026 · Black Rock City, Nevada

Terrible Turtle Camp — co-builder & systems lead at Burning Man

At Burning Man 2026, Shaka is co-builder and systems lead of Terrible Turtle Camp — a 20–30 person theme camp — coordinating a ~270-item inventory across 10 domains (kitchen, shade, water, power, shower, safety, operations) and driving build week from the camp's Boulder HQ at Phoenix Gulch, itself a living land-restoration project of water terraces, native grasses, and drought-tolerant trees.

He built Turtle Ops — a live, open-source (CC0) camp operations platform with real-time inventory tracking, build playbooks, site maps, and onboarding docs, released with a "Fork This Camp" agent kit so any camp on the playa can clone the entire system. He also created Terri, an open-source AI camp assistant who holds the camp's whole knowledge base — logistics, safety, culture, the 10 Principles — and serves campmates in English and Hebrew. Camp infrastructure as a public good. 🐢

The Ambition · India 2026

The P.I.T., ready for India — from gift to protocol

Edge City Goa (October 2026) is the proving ground: the P.I.T. deployed as a forkable open-source template any popup city can adopt — crawl, transcribe, audit, publish — with a target of every session live as a synced transcript+audio experience within 24 hours, consent-ceremony built in. Then Devcon 8 Mumbai, where the transponder meets 5,000+ Ethereum builders at scale. The design is audio-first and low-bandwidth-native — 64kbps audio + glowing text over heavy video — so it works on any connection, any device. The knowledge of these gatherings shouldn't belong only to those who could afford the ticket or the bandwidth.

What funding unlocks: the forkable template documented and packaged, the same-day transcription pipeline hardened for multi-venue capture, the consent-first publishing playbook written for any community to adopt, and localization groundwork for India's linguistic diversity. The system is already running — funding turns a gift into a protocol.

Movement IV · The Broadcast

The Esmeralda P.I.T. — the village that refused to forget

P.I.T. — Public Information Transmission. Named for the orchestra pit where the musicians sit, for the transponder that transmits the village's knowledge — and in honor of OSO P.I.T., Shaka's ETHGlobal Delhi hackathon winner (Most Creative Use of ENS). (Born as "edgeTV"; renamed when that name turned out to belong to existing streaming companies. The pit was always its true name.)

At Edge Esmeralda 2026 — 800+ builders from 90+ countries, one month in Healdsburg — Shaka ran A/V and stewarded the vibe at The Loft, the main talk hall. When the village dissolved, he refused to let a month of world-class knowledge evaporate into a forgotten Drive folder. So he built it a home.

The signature invention is the Knowledge Transponder 🌊 — Shaka's insight that nobody re-watches hour-long conference videos, but people listen. So the P.I.T.'s default mode isn't a video player: it's a meditative listening stage — floating-village artwork, an audio-reactive sunset waveform, and a real-time synced transcript that glows as the words are spoken. Tap any paragraph to jump there. Radio for popup-city knowledge, with a teleprompter soul.

🕸️

Crawl

The village's source Drive was crawled with no API key and no special access — fully reproducible on any public folder, by any village.

🌾

Harvest

173 Whisper transcripts parsed and matched programmatically to their videos, deduped and merged — 166 recordings became 149 true sessions.

🩺

Audit

Every one of 207 files probed for health without bulk downloads. Wifi casualties honestly flagged in the UI — one file literally named "Video Recording Failed" was identified by its transcript and retitled to its real session. Radical honesty as design.

🎁

Publish

A single static site — cinematic weekly browsing, search, in-page playback, synced reading, and four written dispatches distilled from 138 transcripts. No backend. No servers. \$0.

Built by one artist and one AI agent on the Taurus multi-agent platform — persistent agent memory, background worker pipelines, and governance in the loop. When public archiving needed organizer and speaker blessing, Shaka halted his own pipeline within minutes: consent-first publishing, enforced in the workflow — not just the About page. What would normally take a media team and a five-figure budget was done for zero dollars, and gifted open-source to the community: explore the full Esmeralda P.I.T. archive.

Movement V · The Orchestra

The Open Source Orchestra — Ethereum's values, played out loud

OSO is not a brand, not a company, not a logo. It is a movement and a tradition: music as sanctuary, owned by no one, open to everyone — the oldest protocol humanity has, carried into the newest one we're building.

No auditions. No gatekeepers. No conductor on a podium. Every voice is a node; every sound, a contribution. Born at ETH Prague in 2022, OSO has activated the Devcon Music Stage twice — first at Devcon 6 Bogotá, again at Devcon Southeast Asia — and it is, quite literally, the Music Manifesto of the Devcon stage: censorship-resistant (conductorless), open source (Creative Commons), self-owned (every instrument a node), and secure (protecting the vibe of the community within a higher frequency).

🎼

Radical Inclusion

The person who has played for thirty years and the person who has only hummed in the shower — the orchestra needs both, exactly as they are.

🛡️

Conductorless by Design

Censorship resistance as a musical principle. No one calls the tune. We listen to one another — and we all conduct this space together.

🌍

A Living Public Good

OSO turns Ethereum's abstract values into something a room full of strangers can feel in their bodies. That is culture infrastructure.

Movement VII · The Theory

Expansive circularity — the gift that grows

Every grant Shaka has ever received became a gift he gave away bigger. This is not a metaphor — it is the operating system:

"The more I give, the more I receive — the gift expands in a circular expansion. If I constantly need to ask before I offer — if I need a gatekeeper to be my employer — that is a contracting circularity: feeding inward, imploding. I create an expansive circularity — the gift moving outward, growing with every orbit — that will bring global peace, love, and prosperity." — Shaka, The Theory of Circularity

This is why a grant here is never spent — it is planted. The receipts are one scroll up: villages, pits, orchestras, turtles. Every orbit wider than the last.
