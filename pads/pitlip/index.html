The Origin 🌱

How three words became a village instinct

At Edge Esmeralda (Healdsburg, June 2026), Timour — co-founder of Edge City — gave the village an acronym: LIP — Leave It Prettier.

It spread mouth to mouth, town hall to town hall, until it rivaled "leave no trace" itself — because it isn't just about not harming. It's about adding beauty wherever you stand. The space, the town, the conversation, the code, the world: leave it prettier than you found it.

These moments were captured by the Esmeralda P.I.T. — the Public Information Transmission that archived 149 sessions of the village, consent-first, forever. Below: the actual words, pulled straight from the pit's transcripts. The pit provides.
