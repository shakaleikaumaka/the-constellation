VI · The Thesis in One Paragraph

Expansive circularity

If this letter has an investment thesis, it isn't mine — it's my life theory, lived for years before it was ever written down. Here it is, verbatim:

The more I give, the more I receive — the gift expands in a circular expansion. If I constantly need to ask before I offer — if I need a gatekeeper to be my employer — that is a contracting circularity, feeding inward, imploding. I create an expansive circularity — the gift moving outward, growing with every orbit — that will bring global peace, love, and prosperity.

— Shaka's personal theory of circularity, his words, quoted exactly as he speaks them

Read it as economics, because it is. Gatekeeper economies are contracting circularities — platforms that tax the people who make them valuable, permission-walls, approval committees — feeding inward until they implode. The giving economy is the opposite motion, expansive circularity, encoded at the code level: 96.3% of all value flows home to the creator, immutably, and the agents serve, never extract. The whole machine is in the white paper.

All seven of you have backed outsiders before anyone could see them — it's literally the firm. I don't think you'll need a deck to recognize expansive circularity when you read it. You'll just feel which direction the circle is turning.
