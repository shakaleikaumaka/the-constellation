Radical Self-expression

ביטוי עצמי רדיקלי
"Radical self-expression arises from the unique gifts of the individual. No one other than the individual or a collaborating group can determine its content."— from the 10 Principles of Burning Man, written by Larry Harvey (2004)

What it means

  • You decide what you express and how. Art, costume, music, silence, stillness — all valid.
  • Nobody gets to tell you your expression is 'wrong' — and you don't get to tell them theirs is.
  • It's offered as a gift to others — expression, not imposition. Consent matters.

🐢 How the Turtles live it

The Open Source Orchestra is self-expression with no permission needed: pick up an instrument, join the jam, no auditions. The AI Oracle tent is code as canvas. And some turtles express by quietly perfecting a pot of coffee at dawn — that counts too.

First-burn tips

  • You don't have to perform. Being an appreciative audience is participation too.
  • Try one thing you'd never do in the default world. That's what the dust is for.
  • Express, don't impose: your 4am drum solo meets someone else's sleep. Find the right venue.
