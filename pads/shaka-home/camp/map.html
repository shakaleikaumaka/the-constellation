Placement Logic (David-reviewed)

  • Greywater, potable water & bathrooms hug the access road — service trucks reach them without entering camp.
  • Showers pump their own water, so they don't need road access — but ours sit near the water cluster anyway.
  • The semi trailer anchors the Dormitory Shade — our 51' northAmerican moving van (Kentucky Mfg 1996, triple-axle, GVWR 65,000 lb). Keder rail mounts at ~13–13.5', two 20' poles stand 75' downwind, and the Aluminet runs down to ground anchors at ~60°, giving a huge shaded sleeping bay.
  • Cars park behind the semi and along the perimeter — a windbreak and privacy wall.
  • The box truck doubles as a privacy screen and stays drivable for ice runs.
  • AI Oracle tents + Podcast face outward on the public edge — gifts greet the passersby. 🔮
Placement on playa is assigned by BMorg — this layout flexes to fit the actual frontage we get. The logic stays the same even if the shape shifts.
