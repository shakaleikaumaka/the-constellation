III · The Theory of Circularity

A gift with no invoice comes back around.

I've carried a personal theory my whole life — it's the thing underneath the 96.3, the idea the whole giving economy is just an encoding of. I've never put it in a letter before. It belongs in this one, because you are the living proof of it:

Shaka's Theory of Circularity

The more I give, the more I receive — the gift expands in a circular expansion. If I constantly need to ask before I offer — if I need a gatekeeper to be my employer — that is a contracting circularity, feeding inward, imploding. I create an expansive circularity — the gift moving outward, growing with every orbit — that will bring global peace, love, and prosperity.
— my personal theory of circularity, as I've lived it

You didn't ask anyone's permission to make that introduction. There was no gatekeeper standing between your instinct and your gift — you saw something, you opened the door, and you let it go. And look what came back around the circle: an archive, a camp, a white paper, a family, a seat being built at a table. That is expansive circularity — witnessed and documented. The contracting spiral is what happens when giving needs permission: everything feeds inward until it implodes. The expanding one is what you did without even thinking about it.

The numbers everyone asks me about — 96.3, 3.69, 0.01 — are just this theory written in arithmetic. Gifts, not permission slips.
