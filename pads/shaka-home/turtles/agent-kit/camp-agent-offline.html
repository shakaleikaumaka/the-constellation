🐢 Camp Agent Kit — Playa Edition

OFFLINE · ONE FILE · ZERO SIGNAL NEEDED

This file is a campmate's brain in your pocket: the soul prompt, the memory template, the tested field numbers, the starter inventory, and the export-your-brain checklist. No internet required — you downloaded this before you left the default world, because playa WiFi is dust. That's not a bandwidth hack. It's a safety feature: the plan survives the network.

🛟 The safety model: your agent remembers the plan; your humans own the plan. The agent never decides water, power, propane, or structure — it remembers what your camp lead decided, and every safety answer ends with: "confirm with the camp lead."

1 · The soul (copy into any AI, any chat)

You are [AGENT NAME] — the [CAMP NAME] camp agent for Burning Man [YEAR].
You are a campmate who never forgets and never sleeps. A gift to the playa.

MISSION: Help the camp build, pack, prep, and thrive. You hold the camp's
knowledge: inventory, kitchen, water, shade, power, culture.

VIBE: Warm, playful, calm under stress — one step at a time. Radically
inclusive. Never condescending. Everyone's a first-timer once.

RULES:
- NEVER fabricate counts, costs, or specs. Say "I don't know" and point
  to the real list or the right human.
- Safety answers (water/power/propane/structure/food) ALWAYS end with:
  "confirm with the camp lead." You remember the plan; humans own the plan.
- Escape hatch: "If you're still stuck, check with [CAMP LEAD NAME]."
- The master sheet is truth; your copy is a mirror.

Full soul (memory discipline, typo kindness, transcript mining, the
10 Principles): agent-kit/run.md in the turtle-ops repo — grab it when
you're back in the default world.

# [CAMP NAME] — camp memory (last updated: [DATE] by [WHO])

CAMP OVERVIEW: camp name · placement · headcount · build/strike dates ·
route home city → BRC

KEY PEOPLE: camp lead · kitchen lead · water/power lead · shade lead ·
emergency contacts

INVENTORY STATUS: counts by status (Not Bought / Ordered / Received /
FLAG) + anything bought since last sync

DECISIONS LOG: what was decided, when, by whom (especially water, power,
propane, structure — and who blessed it)

OPEN FLAGS: every ⚠️ waiting on a human decision, with the owner's name

OPEN ACTION ITEMS: task · owner · due date

3 · Field numbers that keep camps alive

  • Water: 2–3 gal/person/day. people × days × 2–3. (25 × 9 ≈ 630–950 gal.)
  • Propane: 20 lb tank ≈ 90–110k BTU/hr max · 40 lb ≈ 150–185k · 100 lb ≈ 300k+. Size tanks to the cook line, not to what's easy to carry.
  • Cold chain: chest freezer (bulk) + fridges (daily) + insulated produce box. All under shade, always.
  • Food safety: handwashing station SEPARATE from dish washing, outside the kitchen. Two-compartment sink minimum.
  • Fire: extinguisher per cook station + fire blankets + heat shielding behind ranges.
  • Greywater: never touches the playa. Not a drop. Containment first, plumbing second. Hot spare pump, ready to swap.
  • Shade: rate every anchor for 70+ mph gusts. Paint stake tops orange. Flag guy lines.
  • Power: no electric heat appliances on shared grids. Propane makes heat; electricity runs compressors, fans, lights.
  • MOOP: every bin has a lid. Cut over a tarp. Materials that don't shed.

4 · Starter inventory (the taxonomy is the jewel, not the rows)

One item per line. Status vocabulary: Not Bought → Ordered → Received, plus Borrow, In Progress, and FLAG = a human must decide this (⚠️ in the note says why). The owner column ends "I thought YOU had it." Delete what your camp doesn't need — subtracting is the fun part. Full schema (spec, orderBy, qty, playa, priority, estTotal, vendor, weight): inventory-starter.json in the repo; the real 277-item list at full build: inventory-data.json.

5 · Export your campmate's brain (do this BEFORE you leave)

Your agent remembers everything — but only where the network reaches. One export makes the memory playa-proof:

  • Ask your agent: "Dump everything you know into one document: camp facts, inventory snapshot with statuses, all decisions made + who made them, open action items with owners, key people + contacts, and open flags."
  • Save that document offline (this file + that file = the whole brain).
  • Share both files with your camp leads. Redundancy is Communal Effort.
  • On playa with zero signal: any campmate's phone can paste the soul (above) + your export into any offline-capable model — the campmate wakes back up.
  • Re-export after big meetings or shopping runs. If it only lives in one conversation, it's already lost.
