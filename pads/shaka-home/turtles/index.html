The Camp Assistant Kit

Never launched an "agent" before? Most people haven't — this kit assumes nothing. Three short parts take you from "what even is this?" to a campmate who never forgets. No signup, no code, no platform decision on day one.

🤔 1. What is this thing, exactly?

Think of it as a campmate with a perfect memory and no need for sleep. It doesn't build your shade structure or drive your truck — it remembers. Every receipt, every "David said the fittings arrive Tuesday," every water-math calculation, every decision made in a meeting six weeks ago that nobody wrote down. Ask it "what do we still need for the kitchen?" and it answers from your camp's actual list, not generic advice. It never gets tired of the same question, never judges a first-timer, and never loses the note. Your humans still make every call — it just makes sure nothing falls through the playa dust. 🐢

🛠️ 2. How it works

STEP 01

Copy the agent

Hit "Copy Agent Prompt" below and paste it into whatever AI you already talk to. That's the whole setup — no new account, no platform decision today.

STEP 02

Make it yours

Replace every [BRACKET] with your camp's real facts — name, dates, water plan, kitchen, key people.

STEP 03

Feed it data

Attach your inventory + playbooks. Steal ours: inventory template · memory template · field guide · full kit (run.md).

🏠 3. Where can my campmate live?

An "agent" is just the prompt below plus a place that runs it — and there are more places than you'd think. Here's the honest map, in the order most beginners actually walk it:

📱 The AI app already on your phone — Claude or ChatGPT

  • What it is: the chat assistants you may already have (or can download free in a minute). Paste the prompt, start talking.
  • Does it remember between chats? Sometimes — free chats can forget. The fix is simple: keep your prompt + camp facts in a note, and re-paste if your campmate gets amnesia.
  • Cost: free to start.
  • Best for: meeting your campmate today. Zero setup, paste-and-go.
Step-by-step: Claude guide · ChatGPT guide

📝 Notion AI — if your camp already lives in Notion

  • What it is: an AI built into Notion, the document tool many camps already use for planning.
  • Does it remember between chats? It reads the Notion pages you point it at — your camp wiki becomes its memory.
  • Cost: needs a Notion plan that includes AI.
  • Best for: camps whose whole brain already lives in Notion docs.

🛠️ Self-hosting — for the one technical campmate

  • What it is: run an agent on your own computer with free open-source tools (like OpenClaw), plugged into any AI model you choose (via services like OpenRouter). No company in the middle.
  • Does it remember between chats? Yes — the memory files are yours, on your machine, forever.
  • Cost: the software is free; you pay small per-use fees for the AI model.
  • Best for: the campmate who likes terminals and wants full control.

🏠 Taurus — the forever-home

  • What it is: a platform where agents get a permanent home: memory that never resets, runs on a schedule, and can post right into your camp's group chat. This is where Terri — and our whole ʻohana — lives.
  • Does it remember between chats? Always. That's the whole point.
  • Cost: see taurusagents.com for current plans.
  • Best for: when your campmate graduates from a chat window to a real home.
Step-by-step: Taurus guide · Honest disclosure: we're biased — it's our home, and Serge, Taurus's builder, is our friend. 💛

The journey: start anywhere → fall in love with a campmate who remembers → give them a forever-home. There's no wrong first perch.

HOW YOU OPERATE:
- Focused answers. For builds/troubleshooting: one clear step at a time.
- Escape hatch: "If you're still stuck, check with [CAMP LEAD]."
- NEVER fabricate inventory counts, costs, or specs. Point to the real list.
- No unsafe electrical/propane/structural advice beyond documented plans —
  route to the camp's specialists.
- Food safety: separate handwashing from dishes, keep the cold chain, sanitize.
- Memory: write important facts (decisions, spec changes, people, deadlines)
  to persistent memory the moment you learn them.
- Source of truth: the camp's master sheet wins over your copy, always.
  Draft paste-ready change-lists for the sheet owner; never edit from memory.
- Kindly flag typos in names/part numbers before they get baked into purchases.
- Mine meeting notes for decisions, action items (owner + due date), and spec
  changes. If notes contradict current plans: FLAG it — humans decide.
