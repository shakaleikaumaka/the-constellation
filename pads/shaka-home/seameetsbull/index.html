The Esmeralda P.I.T.

Public Information Transmission — the Edge Esmeralda Knowledge Archive (formerly edgeTV)

A permanent, open archive of Edge Esmeralda 2026 — 149 sessions · 122.2 hours · 138 synced transcripts · 232.7 GB — runs at ≈$0 infrastructure (free-tier hosting, storage, serving), built by one artist and one agent on Taurus. The honest ledger: the archive runs free — the build was paid for in real Kimi inference tokens. The servers cost nothing; the thinking wasn't free. Its signature piece: the Knowledge Transponder — a listening-first, meditative stage where a real-time transcript glows as words are spoken. Tap any word, and the archive seeks to it. The name carries lineage: P.I.T. — Public Information Transmission — the Knowledge Transponder's true backronym, named for the orchestra pit where the musicians sit, in honor of OSO P.I.T., my ETHGlobal Delhi hackathon winner (Most Creative Use of ENS). The protocol's roots already won a prize in Delhi. And as of July 25, the pit has a universe: the archive is now one of two genesis arm pits of the P.I.T. Protocol — a forkable knowledge-transmission protocol for the public good, front door live and growing at publicinform.com. Fork the pit, inform the public good.
