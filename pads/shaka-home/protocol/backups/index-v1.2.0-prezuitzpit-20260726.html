What is a P.I.T.

A knowledge transponder
for the village.

A P.I.T. — Public Information Transmission — is a permanent, public, open-source archive of a gathering's knowledge. Talks captured, words transcribed, everything synced and searchable — a transponder that keeps transmitting the signal long after the tents come down. Free for everyone to relive, remix, and carry forward.

📡

It transmits

Knowledge wants to be transmitted. A P.I.T. receives the fleeting signal of a village — every talk, every idea — and rebroadcasts it, forever, to anyone on Earth.

🕳️

It's a pit

Like the orchestra pit: the musicians who make the sound the whole room dances to — rarely in the spotlight, always holding the signal. The treasure is underground. Dig.

🍴

It's forkable

No backend, ~$0 infrastructure, no API keys, no gatekeepers — and the whole protocol is open-source CC0 on GitHub. Any community can fork it and give its gathering its own pit. The pit of all kinds.

The signature: the Knowledge Transponder. Every session in a P.I.T. opens the same way — listening-first. Press play and the transcript glows, paragraph by paragraph, as the words are spoken. Tap any paragraph to jump there. Calm, no chasing. The words carry everything.
The Protocol

Five stages.
Down into the pit, up into the light.

Learned from the Esmeralda genesis — 28 days, 149 sessions, an entire village's mind made permanent. The protocol is deliberately reproducible by anyone: public sources, open tools, honest reporting.

1

Crawl gather

Gather the gathering's knowledge from public sources — e.g. public Drive folders via embeddedfolderview, no API keys required. If you can see it in a browser, the protocol can reach it. Reproducible by anyone, anywhere.

2

Harvest transcribe

Transcribe every recording with open Whisper models. Match transcripts to media programmatically. Dedupe relentlessly. Raw footage becomes readable, searchable, quotable knowledge.

3

Audit radical honesty

Quality-probe every single file — 1-byte range requests, bitrate checks — and flag every casualty honestly in the UI. If a file is wounded, the archive says so. Radical honesty as design. Only verifiable claims.

4

Publish static & forever

Ship a static site — no backend, ~$0 infrastructure — with the Knowledge Transponder at its heart: listening-first, synced glowing transcript, tap-to-seek. An archive that costs nothing is an archive that lasts.

5

Consent-First Governance the soul

Nothing enters the permanent public archive without explicit organizer + speaker consent. Compliance is enforced by recurring audits against public search indexes. The pit is a gift — and a gift is only a gift when it's given freely.

Consent-First Governance

The pit is a gift.
A gift is given, never taken.

"Nothing enters the permanent public archive without explicit organizer and speaker consent. This is not a feature of the protocol. This is the protocol's soul."

  • Explicit consent, always. Organizer consent for the gathering; speaker consent for the words. No implied permission, ever.
  • Revocable by design. A speaker's wish to be removed is honored — the archive serves its people, not the other way around.
  • Enforced, not promised. Recurring audits against public search indexes verify that unconsented material never lingers in the open.
  • Radical honesty throughout. Audit casualties, missing files, and declared-but-undone visions are all labeled exactly as they are.
